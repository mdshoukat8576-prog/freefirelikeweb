POLICY.

How FFLike handles customer order details, game UIDs, and payment verification receipts.

Privacy Summary at a Glance:

FFLike collects only the minimum information required to deliver your ordered profile likes: your public numeric Free Fire UID, selected regional server, bank transaction reference (UTR), and payment screenshot. We never request or collect game passwords, OTPs, social logins, or personal banking credentials.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order on FFLike, we collect only the necessary operational data:

  • Free Fire Player UID: Your public numeric game ID to which likes are delivered.
  • Regional Game Server: To ensure likes are routed to the proper regional cluster.
  • Payment Verification Proof: The transaction screenshot and 12-digit UPI UTR reference number you submit.
  • Optional Telegram Handle: If you choose to provide your Telegram username for order updates.

2. Strict "No Password" Security Guarantee

FFLike operates on a strictly non-invasive basis. We will never ask for your Free Fire account password, Google/Facebook/VK login details, OTP codes, or account recovery information. If anyone claiming to represent FFLike asks for your password, do not provide it and report them immediately to our official Telegram support.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your details are used exclusively for:

  • Reconciling manual UPI banking receipts against incoming order amounts.
  • Queuing your game UID for daily like distribution.
  • Displaying accurate tracking status on our public Order Status portal via your unique Order ID.
  • Providing personalized customer assistance if you open a support ticket on Telegram.

4. Data Storage & Protection

Order records and payment proof images are stored on secured server directories with restricted administrative access. Sensitive server configurations (such as Telegram bot tokens and admin verification keys) are secured within server-side environment variables and are never transmitted to the browser or client-side JavaScript.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose customer order details to any third-party marketing companies, ad networks, or data brokers. Information is shared only with our designated operations staff via secured Telegram webhooks strictly for transaction authorization.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Order records are maintained temporarily to allow customers to verify past orders on the Order Status page. You may request the permanent deletion of your UID and order records from our active database at any time by sending your Order ID to our support desk at @riyaznx.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect operational improvements. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date. If you have questions regarding our privacy practices, contact us via Telegram at @riyaznx ↗.
