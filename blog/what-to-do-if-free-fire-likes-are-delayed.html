WHAT TO DO IF LIKES
ARE DELAYED.

A quick resolution guide covering order tracking, UID corrections, server maintenance, and refund guarantees.

Published: Oct 14, 2026 • Read Time: 4 mins • Support & Help

Step 1: Check Your Real-Time Order Status

Every order on FFLike receives an identifier like FF-XXXXX-XXXXX. Navigate to our Order Status Page and enter your ID to check its current lifecycle stage:

  • awaiting_payment: The order was initiated, but the UTR number or screenshot has not been uploaded yet.
  • proof_submitted: Payment proof is uploaded. Our human team verifies payments instantly upon receipt.
  • processing: Verification is complete. 200 likes are queued and actively dispatching in 24-hour cycles.
  • completed: All scheduled daily likes for your selected duration have been successfully delivered.

Step 2: Check for Common Delay Factors

1

Did You Submit the Correct Numeric UID?

If you mistyped a digit when ordering, the system may have attempted to send likes to an invalid or different account. Check the UID listed on your Order Status screen.

2

Did the Regional Server Match Your Account?

Garena restricts cross-server delivery. If an account registered on the India (IND) server was ordered under the Brazil (BR) server, the delivery will pause until the server setting is corrected.

3

Is Garena Running Scheduled Server Maintenance (OB Patch)?

Every few months, Garena Free Fire takes global game servers offline for 6 to 9 hours to deploy major OB updates (e.g., OB46, OB47). During game patch maintenance, all in-game profile updates pause globally. Daily delivery resumes automatically once servers go live.
