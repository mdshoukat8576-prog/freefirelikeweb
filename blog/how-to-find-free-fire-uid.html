HOW TO FIND YOUR
FREE FIRE UID.

A quick, foolproof tutorial to locate and copy your numeric Free Fire player ID in seconds without risk.

Published: Oct 14, 2026 • Read Time: 3 mins • Account Setup

Why Your Free Fire UID Is Important

In Free Fire, every account is assigned a permanent, unique numerical identifier known as the UID (User ID). While you can change your in-game nickname repeatedly using Name Change Cards or diamonds, your UID never changes throughout the life of your account.

When ordering daily profile likes on FFLike, your numeric UID is the only information needed to route likes directly to your in-game profile. We never require your password, social login, or security codes.

Step-by-Step: Copying Your UID in Free Fire

1

Open Free Fire and Enter Main Lobby

Launch the game on your Android or iOS device and make sure you are logged into your primary player profile in the main game lobby.

2

Tap Your Profile Banner in the Top-Left

In the top-left corner of the lobby screen, you will see your player avatar, frame, and current level badge. Tap on this area to open your full Player Profile card.

3

Navigate to the Basic Info Section

On the left-hand profile menu, select the Basic tab. Here you will see your player avatar, signature, guild banner, and account stats.

4

Locate the UID and Tap the Copy Button

Directly beneath your displayed nickname, you will see a field labelled UID: [numbers]. Tap the small yellow/white dual-rectangle copy icon beside the number. You will see an on-screen toast confirming the UID was copied to your clipboard.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Copying your Nickname instead of UID: Many players enter their display name (e.g., ꧁༺SHADOW༻꧂) instead of their numeric ID. Displays can change or contain invisible unicode characters; our automated delivery system only accepts digits (5 to 15 numbers).
  • Confusing Guild ID with Player UID: Your Guild ID belongs to your team, not your personal character. Ensure you copy the ID from your personal profile screen.
  • Typing Manually: Mistyping a single digit will send profile likes to another player's profile. Always use the in-game copy button and paste directly into the order field.

🛡️ Strict Security & Account Safety Rule

Your Free Fire UID is public information visible to any player who inspects your profile or matches with you. It is 100% safe to share. However, never share your game password, Google/Facebook login, or two-factor OTP with anyone. FFLike strictly operates via public UID only.
