QUESTIONS
ABOUT LIKES.

Everything customers need to know before placing a Free Fire Auto Like order.

How many likes do I get each day?

Each plan is based on 200 likes per day. The total likes shown on the plan are calculated from the selected number of days.

Which UID should I enter?

Enter the Free Fire UID of the account that should receive the likes. Check the UID carefully before creating the order.

Which server should I select?

Select the server/region used by the Free Fire account. If you are unsure, contact Telegram support before paying.

How does payment work?

Create the order first. The checkout then shows the exact amount, QR and UPI details. Pay that amount and submit your UTR/reference plus a payment screenshot.

What happens after I submit proof?

Your order changes to proof submitted and waits for manual payment verification. The owner can then move it to processing or completed.

Can I track my Auto Like order?

Yes. Save the Order ID shown during checkout and use the Order Status page to see its current status.

What if I entered the wrong UID?

Contact the owner on Telegram as soon as possible with your Order ID. Do not create another payment until the owner confirms what to do.
