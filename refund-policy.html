REFUND, DELAY &
SUPPORT POLICY.

Honest, transparent guidelines on payment verification, delivery timelines, order adjustments, and refund guarantees.

Summary of FFLike Refund Terms:

FFLike provides 100% full UPI refunds if we are technically unable to deliver daily profile likes to a confirmed, valid Free Fire numeric player UID. Order verification and delivery queue placement occur promptly upon valid proof submission. Support is available 24/7 via Telegram to resolve any payment, UID, or delivery inquiries.

1. Payment Verification & Processing

FFLike utilizes a manual QR payment flow to keep prices transparent with zero gateway surcharges. Once you submit your 12-digit bank UTR reference and transaction screenshot, our operations team verifies the record. Verification is processed promptly, and your UID enters the active delivery roster.

2. Delivery Expectations & Schedule

Every active plan provides exactly 200 Free Fire profile likes per calendar day. Daily batches commence once payment proof is verified. Because likes are delivered via daily scheduled queue batches rather than an unnatural one-second spike, your profile builds social credibility organically across the duration of your plan.

3. Delivery Delay Protocols

In rare situations, delivery may be temporarily delayed due to:

  • Free Fire game client patch updates or emergency server maintenance by Garena.
  • Unclear payment screenshots or mismatched bank UTR numbers requiring manual customer confirmation.
  • Regional server connectivity fluctuations.

If your order status displays "Processing" but your in-game profile like counter has not updated after 24 hours, contact our 24/7 Telegram support team with your Order ID for immediate investigation.

4. 100% Full Refund Guarantee

We stand behind our delivery commitments. You are entitled to a 100% full refund back to your original UPI payment method under the following circumstances:

  • Technical Inability to Deliver: If FFLike cannot initiate profile likes to your valid, existing Free Fire UID due to technical or system limitations.
  • Duplicate Transaction: If your banking app charged you multiple times for the same Order ID.
  • Unfulfillable Regional Server: If your selected server cannot be serviced by our delivery network.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds cannot be granted under the following circumstances:

  • Incorrect UID Provided by Customer: If likes were already delivered to an incorrect numeric UID entered during checkout. (Please verify your UID carefully before paying).
  • Completed Deliveries: Once daily likes have successfully registered on the profile counter.
  • Change of Mind: After order activation and queue processing has begun.

6. Incorrect UID Correction Procedure

If you realize that you entered a typo in your UID or selected the wrong regional server, contact support immediately on Telegram at @riyaznx. If your order has not yet entered active delivery, we will update your UID free of charge.

7. How to Request a Refund or Order Investigation

To request an investigation or refund, contact our 24/7 support channel with the following four details:

  1. Your FFLike Order ID (e.g., FF-XXXXX-XXXXX).
  2. Your numeric Free Fire UID and game server.
  3. The 12-digit UPI UTR / Transaction Reference.
  4. A clear screenshot of your payment receipt.

Approved refunds are remitted directly to the original UPI VPA within 24 hours of confirmation.
